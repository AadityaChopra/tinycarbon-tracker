Preface

The Online and Distance Learning programs of Shoolini University are designed to provide flexible, accessible, and high-quality education. Students can learn from anywhere at their own pace without compromising the quality of education.

With digital platforms, live classes, recorded lectures, and continuous assessments, learning becomes interactive, convenient, and student-friendly. The curriculum focuses on both theory and practical understanding.

This preface highlights the mission of Shoolini University's Online and Distance Learning Program – to make quality education accessible, affordable, and flexible for all learners.
